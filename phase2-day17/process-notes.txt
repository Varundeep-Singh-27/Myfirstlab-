PROCESS = Server pe chal raha ek kaam
Har process ka ek PID (ID number) hota hai

Commands:
1. ps aux = Saare processes dekho
2. ps aux | grep nginx = sirf nginx wala dhoondo
3. top = Live dekho kaun kitni RAM/CPU kha raha hai (q se bahar)
4. kill PID = us kaam ko band karo
5. kill -9 PID = zabardasti band karo (force)
6. jobs = background ke kaam dekho
7. & = koi kaam background me chalao
8. fg = background wale ko aage lao

Practical Termux pe:
- sleep 100 & = background me 100 sec so jao
- jobs = background jobs dekho
- kill %1 = job number 1 ko band karo
- ps aux | grep sleep = check karo sleep chal raha hai kya
- killall sleep = saare sleep band
